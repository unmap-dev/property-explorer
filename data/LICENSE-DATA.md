# Licence of the sample data

`data/listings.sample.json` is **fictional**: every listing's type, price, bedrooms, bathrooms,
floor area and year built is made up by `scripts/make-listings.mjs`. Those figures are part of
this repository and under its MIT licence.

What the file takes from a real source is where the listings sit: a point inside each of 25
community districts, and that district's name and sector, derived from **The City of Calgary,
Community District Boundaries**, published on Open Calgary:
<https://data.calgary.ca/Base-Maps/Community-District-Boundaries/surr-xmvs>

That information is used under the **Open Government Licence – City of Calgary** (version 2.1 when
fetched): <https://data.calgary.ca/d/Open-Data-Terms/u45n-7awa>. In short (read the text there):
you may copy, modify and distribute it for any lawful purpose, commercial or not, with the
attribution below; it grants no right to the City's names or logos and suggests no endorsement;
it is provided as is, without warranty.
